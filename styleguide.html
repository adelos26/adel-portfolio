The Idea

I Am a Tall Woman is a solo project from my third year in Bezalel’s Illustration to Animation program. The brief was an animated Op-Doc built on an audio clip. I chose a segment from Kan 11’s “Kan Mekshivim,” in which Tahel tells stories in a vivid, theatrical voice. When she said “tall people are lanky,” I knew it was the one.

My question was how to convey feeling different in a light way, without leaning on literal visuals. I designed a minimalist, faceless figure with a small head and long limbs, and used transitions to replace literal descriptions with visual ideas.
